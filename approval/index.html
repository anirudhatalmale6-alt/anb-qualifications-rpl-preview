Part B — how your two qualifications are described

These descriptions do not name Triscott, but they describe your qualifications, so we would rather you checked them for accuracy than have us guess.

10. Certificate III card

CHC33021 Certificate III in Individual Support — to get into care work. The standard entry qualification for aged care, home care and disability support. This is the one most employers ask for before they will put you on. Structure: 15 units, 9 core and 6 electives. Work placement: 120 hours; if you already work in care, your current work may count. Specialisations: Ageing, Disability, or Ageing and Disability.

11. Certificate IV card

CHC43015 Certificate IV in Ageing Support — to step up in aged care. The next level for people already working in aged care who want more responsibility: care planning, coordinating support, supervising and mentoring other workers. Structure: 18 units, 15 core and 3 electives. Work placement: 120 hours; your current aged care work may count. Where it leads: senior personal care worker, team leader, care supervisor and similar roles.

How the two roles are kept apart

  • A & B's own RTO number (46055) appears only alongside its own first aid and CPR certificates, never alongside CHC33021 or CHC43015.
  • Full national codes and titles are used throughout — never an abbreviated or invented course name.

  • No claim about A & B's past referral numbers is attached to Triscott's name.
