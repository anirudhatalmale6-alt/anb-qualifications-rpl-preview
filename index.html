Common questions

Is the qualification actually nationally recognised?

Yes. It is issued by a Registered Training Organisation against a qualification on the national register, and it carries the same weight as one earned by sitting the full course.

Does A&B issue these certificates?

No. A&B First Aid Training issues its own first aid and CPR certificates only. The qualifications on this page are assessed and issued by the partner RTO named at the top of this page. We handle your enrolment, guidance and support.

What if I have gaps in my evidence?

That is normal. The assessor identifies exactly what is missing and you complete targeted gap training for that part only — not the whole qualification.

How long does it take?

It depends how quickly you gather your evidence. Most people are done within a few weeks to a few months of starting.
