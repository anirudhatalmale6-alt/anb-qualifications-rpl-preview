Two ways to get it

Which one applies to you depends entirely on whether you are already working in the sector.

Recognition of Prior Learning

Already working in care
  • You are already doing the work — personal care, home care, aged care, disability support.
  • Instead of sitting the course, you prove what you already do: payslips, position description, a supervisor reference, work samples.
  • The college's assessor reviews your evidence and may have a short competency conversation with you.
  • Anything genuinely missing is filled with targeted gap training — just that part, not the whole qualification.
  • Usually the faster and cheaper route if your experience is current.

The full course

New to the industry
  • You complete all 15 units — 9 core plus 6 electives — with the college.
  • Includes the mandatory 120 hours of work placement in a real care setting.
  • The college arranges and supervises the placement.
  • You finish job-ready with the same nationally recognised qualification.
  • The right path if you have no paid experience in care yet.

Choose your specialisation

CHC33021 can be awarded with a specialisation on the certificate. It comes down to which electives you do.

Ageing

Residential aged care and home care for older people.

Disability

Supporting people living with disability, including NDIS settings.

Ageing and Disability

Both, which keeps the most doors open when you apply for work.

Common questions

Who actually gives me the certificate?

[College Name], RTO code [XXXXX]. They deliver the training, do the assessing and issue the qualification. A&B First Aid Training refers and enrols you with them. We do not issue this qualification — we only issue our own first aid and CPR certificates.

Is it the same certificate as doing it directly with the college?

Yes, identical. Same code, same college, same nationally recognised qualification. Going through us just means someone walks you through it instead of leaving you to work it out alone.

Does it cost more to go through you?

No. You pay the college's normal fee.

I have been in aged care for years — do I really have to do the whole course?

Probably not. That is exactly what Recognition of Prior Learning is for. Do the pathway check above and we will tell you where you stand.

How long does RPL take?

It depends how quickly you gather your evidence. The assessment itself is not the slow part — collecting payslips, your position description and a supervisor reference usually is.
